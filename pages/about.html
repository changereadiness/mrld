ABOUT MRLD

A Canadian company building the bridge between global supply and real market opportunity.

MRLD was built around a simple observation: capable manufacturers do not always have direct access to the markets where their equipment can create value, while buyers do not always have access to the suppliers best suited to their requirements.   We operate between those two sides of the market. We develop relationships with selected manufacturers, identify commercial opportunities, and help connect equipment with qualified buyers.   Our role is not to be everything to everyone; we focus on building a focused network of manufacturers, buyers, and specialized partners around equipment categories where market access, commercial representation, and execution matter.

FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Ali Saïd

Ali Saïd brings a background in business management, organizational leadership, technology, change management, and international business development.

His work has consistently centred on understanding complex requirements, bringing the right people together, and turning opportunities into practical next steps.

At MRLD, that experience takes a more commercial and hands-on form: developing manufacturer relationships, identifying market opportunities, supporting equipment sourcing, and coordinating the stakeholders required to move projects forward.

MRLD is intentionally being built as a focused business rather than a traditional equipment catalogue — combining market access, relationships, and execution around selected industrial equipment categories.
