FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Ali Saïd

Technology · Business Development · Transformation

Ali’s background spans technology, international business development and organizational transformation, including roles with Apple and Holcim.

MRLD grew from a simple observation: capable manufacturers do not always have direct access to the markets where their equipment can create value, while buyers do not always have access to the suppliers best suited to their requirements.

At MRLD, that experience takes a deliberately focused commercial form: developing relationships with selected manufacturers, identifying opportunities, and connecting equipment with qualified buyers.

The approach is practical: understand the requirement, identify the right people, and move the opportunity forward.

ONE OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Selective by design.

MRLD works with manufacturers, buyers and opportunities where there is a credible commercial fit on both sides.
