Privacy Policy

MRLD respects the privacy of individuals who interact with our website and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

Our Privacy Practices

This Privacy Policy explains how MRLD collects, uses, protects, and manages personal information submitted through this website.

01. Who We Are

MRLD is a Canadian company and a business of Sayudi Inc. MRLD operates in the industrial equipment and commercial representation sector, connecting selected manufacturers, suppliers, buyers, and project partners across markets.

For questions concerning privacy or the handling of personal information, you may contact us using the information below.

02. Information We Collect

MRLD may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us or submit an inquiry through this website.

Depending on the nature of your inquiry, this may include:

  • Your name
  • Company or organization name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Industry and project information
  • Equipment or logistics requirements
  • Other information you choose to provide in your message

We seek to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is requested.

03. How We Use Personal Information

Personal information submitted to MRLD may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Understand equipment, sourcing, or project requirements
  • Evaluate potential business opportunities
  • Communicate with prospective clients, manufacturers, suppliers, and partners
  • Coordinate services or project-related activities
  • Maintain business records and communications
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations

MRLD does not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the purpose for which it was collected unless permitted or required by law.

04. Disclosure of Personal Information

MRLD does not sell or rent personal information.

Where reasonably necessary to respond to an inquiry or support a project, information may be shared with service providers, suppliers, logistics providers, manufacturers, professional advisers, or other parties involved in the relevant business activity.

Information may also be disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law.

Some service providers used to operate this website or process communications may store or process information outside Canada or outside the jurisdiction in which you reside.

05. Website Forms and Service Providers

MRLD uses third-party service providers to operate certain website functions, including the processing and delivery of contact form submissions.

Information submitted through an MRLD contact form may therefore be transmitted to the third-party service provider responsible for processing that submission.

These providers may process or store information in jurisdictions outside Canada. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

06. Cookies and Website Technologies

MRLD may use basic technologies that are necessary for the operation, security, and functionality of this website.

At this time, MRLD does not intentionally use this website to collect sensitive personal information through tracking technologies.

If additional analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies are introduced, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those practices.

07. Protection of Personal Information

MRLD takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or storage over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08. Retention of Personal Information

MRLD retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate business records, or to meet legal or regulatory requirements.

When personal information is no longer required, it may be securely deleted, destroyed, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable requirements.

09. Access and Correction

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access to personal information held by MRLD about them and may request correction of information that is inaccurate or incomplete.

Requests concerning personal information should be directed to the contact identified below.

10. Consent

By voluntarily providing personal information through this website, you acknowledge that MRLD may collect, use, and disclose that information for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable law.

Where consent is required, individuals may have the right to withdraw their consent, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice.

11. Privacy Inquiries

Questions, requests, or concerns regarding the handling of personal information may be directed to:

MRLD / Sayudi Inc.
Canada
Email: contact@sayudi.com

MRLD will review privacy inquiries and respond within a reasonable period, subject to applicable requirements.

12. Updates to This Policy

MRLD may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to its practices, services, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

The most recent version will be published on this page.
