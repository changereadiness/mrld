02 · HOW WE WORK

A practical commercial relationship.

MRLD develops selective relationships around real capability and real opportunity. The structure should fit the business at hand rather than force every partner into the same model.

01

Opportunity Development

Identify and qualify buyers, projects, or commercial needs where a partner's capability may be relevant.

02

Sales Representation

Where appropriate, support defined products, customers, markets, or opportunities under a mutually agreed commercial framework.

03

Sourcing & Alternatives

Help qualified buyers identify suitable equipment, supply options, or manufacturer alternatives when there is a genuine requirement.

04

Project Coordination

Coordinate manufacturers, buyers, logistics providers, documentation, and project requirements when MRLD can add value to execution.

05

Commercial Support

Support proposals, follow-up, stakeholder coordination, and information flow without pretending to replace the technical or contractual authority of the manufacturer.

06

Opportunity-Specific Work

Work around a single customer or project when a broader representation arrangement would add unnecessary structure.

04 · WHAT WE NEED

Enough information to act.

MRLD does not need an exhaustive vendor package at the beginning. These are the items that most often determine whether we can progress efficiently.

✓Legal company name, jurisdiction, and primary business contact
✓Relevant product families, applications, and technical documentation
✓Certifications, warranty approach, and any material product restrictions
✓Typical lead times, shipping origin, and export / delivery capabilities
✓Existing representation, channel, customer, or market restrictions that matter
✓The commercial objective: broad relationship, defined market, specific customer, or single opportunity
✓Enough pricing or commercial context to know how a real opportunity should be handled
✓An authorized person who can approve commercial terms when discussions become concrete
